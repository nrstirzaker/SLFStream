Sunday 4th October, starting at 10 am
Sophie was murdered in 2007 at the age of 20 because she was different, a Goth. Sylvia her mother created the Foundation using her daughter's name for the acronym Stamp Out Prejudice, Hatred and Interolerance Everywhere. The Foundation, in Sophie's memory, works in schools to combat hatred and bullying
Horizon Zero Dawn is the inspiring story of Aloy, outcast as an infant and shunned as she grows. She battles prejudice, cults and killer machines to uncover the mystery of her birth and discovers a unique purpose that she must fulfil or all will be lost
